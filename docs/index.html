Gather the Embers. Save the Cosmos.

About the Game

In a shattered cosmos once unified by the Divine Aurora Virelia, an ancient Void splintered all life. You are the prophesied Flameborne, reborn from the ashes of the Fallen Star in Verdant Hollow. Your quest: gather five scattered embers from realms of ice, magma, astral light, and echoing time. Along the way, forge alliances and face impossible choices: protect civilization’s fragile hope, or seize the embers’ power to reshape the universe under your will. Every puzzle solved and enemy vanquished brings you closer to rekindling Virelia—or plunging existence into eternal shadow.

Features

Dynamic Classes

Choose from Warriors, Mages, Archers, and more — each with unique skill trees.

Environmental Puzzles

Manipulate time, gravity, fire, and elemental cycles to unlock secrets.

Branching Narrative

Critical mid-game choices reshape allies, environments, and ending.

Epic Boss Fights

Confront powerful guardians in each realm — and your own fate.

Game Worlds

Verdant Hollow (Forest)

Overgrown ruins and bioluminescent flora hide the First Ember beneath ancient temples.

Frostbound Sanctuary (Ice)

Glacial islands under aurora-lit skies, where frost and light converge.

Obsidian Depths (Magma)

Molten rivers and runic forges test your heat resistance and puzzle acumen.

Celestial Ruins (Astral)

Floating spires in low gravity challenge your agility and courage.

Veil of Echoes (Temporal)

A shifting realm of past and future, where memory is both ally and foe.
